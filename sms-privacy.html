SMS Privacy Policy

Brand: Justin Dunaway

Program: property camera alerts

Last updated: 2026-10-09

This policy covers text messages from Justin Dunaway to the account owner's own mobile phone. The messages are operational alerts from the owner's UniFi Protect cameras, such as motion or an animal on the property. They are not marketing, promotions, or messages to the public.

Information used

  • The mobile number that opted in to these alerts
  • Opt-in, opt-out, and help consent records for that number
  • Message delivery status needed to send the alert

How it is used

The mobile number is used only to send the camera alerts the owner requested, and to honor STOP and HELP replies. Message frequency varies with camera activity. Message and data rates may apply.

No sale or sharing

Mobile numbers, message opt-in data, and consent information are not sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. They are not used for affiliate marketing or lead generation.

Opt out

Reply STOP to end these texts. Reply HELP for help. Opting out stops further camera alerts to that number.
